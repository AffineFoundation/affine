====================================================================================================
6. VERDICT
====================================================================================================
1. Deliberation buys CONFIDENCE, not agreement between draws: v1 pairwise same-class 0.65 (norm-exact 0.12, Jaccard>=.5 0.41) vs the teacher's own 0.55 (exact 0.12); the k=10 modal class holds 0.76 of samples, >=0.5 at 27/30 states.
2. Confidence is not accuracy: on the 18 ground-truth split+ceiling states the v1 modal class is correct 0 / wrong 10 / mixed 7 / novel 1 (accuracy 0.00); confident (>=0.5) states are no better (0/17 correct).
3. The deliberated frontier converges on the TEACHER's decision class (24/30 states; direct, chain-free 21/30) — at 9/11 ceiling states that class is one the teacher already failed with, at the other 2 it is 'mixed' (the judge equates the solved F1 proposal with the teacher's failed action); no ceiling state has a modal class that is cleanly a solved action.
4. The easy 'outcome' states (12 states, mostly all-solved) inflate pooled accuracy to 0.42; critique (v2) and self-critique (v3) raise modal share to 0.84/0.82 and add novel actions (v2 direct novel 13/30) without lifting accuracy on the hard 18.
5. A per-state frontier oracle from deliberation is NOT viable as the ranking target on this evidence: it would rank miners by similarity to a confident consensus that is the teacher's own failure mode at exactly the states where the teacher fails; only environment-verified continuations separate solved from failed first actions, and even those separate only at norm-exact granularity.
