== 7. VERDICT ==
1. Separation today: FT-match separates acting from not-acting (king-ls +0.625 z 18.3; king-repeat +0.406 z 8.9) but barely separates real policies: teacher-king 0.057 (z 2.1, sign p 0.35), challenger-king 0.050 (p 0.19), king-genesis 0.026 (p 0.72); per-turn SD of T ~0.47 -> the paired 2-sigma bar at n=1300 is ~0.037, so the teacher-king gap would clear it and the live king-challenger gaps would not.
2. What the targets are: 71% of teacher ref actions already sit in the frontier's modal class (60% tool_call .. 87% text); the modal class holds 0.85 of frontier samples (1.61 classes/turn). On live D turns a frontier target set is ~70% 'what the teacher does anyway' plus a ~30% frontier-only decision.
3. Is the target right where truth exists (30 graded states): frontier modal class is a solved class 67.9% vs teacher modal 60.0%; a random frontier sample's class solves 0.47 vs a random teacher action 0.45; execution-verified ceiling identical (70.0% both). On the 11 teacher-fails states the frontier modal touches a solve on 3 vs 2 states; harvest: 1/32 forced frontier proposals rescued a failed state. The frontier's target is about as right as the teacher's, not more.
4. Panel: mean_T tracks the agentic axis (rho 0.71, p 0.012, n=12) but the turn-controlled excess does not reach significance (rho 0.50, p 0.096); kings 11-15 sit ~-0.16 under the teacher refs, 16-20 ~-0.04 — consistent with, not proof of, the mechanism; no king was trained on these targets, so a null here would not falsify the frame.
5. Ceiling: published frontier margins over the teacher are large on agentic axes (TB2 +17..+38, tau2-telecom +23 DS-V4, LCB +17..+30, SWE-V +2..+17 with harness caveats, GPQA +9..+10, IFBench +13; tau2 airline/retail, BFCL, AIME unreported) — but the internal proxy inverts: GLM-5.2 as datagen policy is BELOW the teacher on our own coding/terminal sources (swesmith -13.6, multiswe -11.9, swerebench -9.6, TB2 -8.9 pts; math +0.9). The affordable target generator (GLM-5.x via Engy) is not above the teacher on D's tasks.
6. A miner trained to reproduce these targets: on the ~70% agreeing turns it learns the teacher (what min(R,G) already rewards); on the ~30% divergent turns it learns GLM-5.3's decision, whose measured truth rate equals the teacher's. Plausible gains: format/decision compliance on text and terminus (modal 0.85-0.94, but the teacher is already credited 86-98% there) and whatever GLM-5.3's SWE/terminal edge is on tasks where it IS stronger — not demonstrable from D's own traces today. Cost $0.030/turn ($194/day fold, $18,918 for all D) is affordable; the binding constraint is the generator's margin, not the price.
