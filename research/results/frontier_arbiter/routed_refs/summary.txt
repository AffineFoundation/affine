TERMS (one line each)
  z_R    centred tempered LME (tau 0.03) of the miner thought's lift on the REF actions, lpC(y_ref|x,z_miner)-lpC(y_ref|x,0),
         minus the arm's LOO anchor mean, over the arm x dialect pooled LOO sd  ("does the thought predict the refs' actions")
  z_A    tempered LME of the summed lift of the MINER action under each REF thought, [lpC(y_miner|x,z_ref)-lpC(y_miner|x,0)]*bytes,
         anchored/standardised the same way  ("would the teacher, thinking the ref's thought, take this action")
  typ_c  2 - |m_c - mu_c|/sigma_c where m_c = content-masked (theta 1.0) mean token logprob of the miner thought under Qwen;
         anchor mu_c/sigma_c ALWAYS from the stored Qwen refs (LOO for a Qwen ref itself); >= 0 = "in band"
  min    turn score = min(z_R, typ_c, z_A); "bind" = which leg is the minimum
  arms   Q3 = 3 stored Qwen refs (today's meter); G3 = 3 fresh Genesis (Qwen3.6-35B-A3B) refs; MIX = 2 Qwen + 1 Genesis;
         O3 = 3 fresh Occamy (Accio-Lab/occamy-1.0) refs.  All echoes under Qwen3.8-27B (rented vLLM pod, echo-cache plugin).

         genesis_fresh = 4th Genesis sample (never a ref); occamy_fresh = 4th Occamy sample; hybrid_qz_ga = VIRTUAL miner
         "think like Qwen, do what Genesis does" = stored teacher thought q_j + genesis_fresh action (scored from existing echoes,
         LOO on q_j when it is a ref).  Family rows are means over members; paired = per-turn difference of family means.

VERDICT
 1. No headroom for the member itself where the env says it is better: with Genesis refs under Qwen echoes on tau2, Genesis-as-miner
    ties the teacher held-out (+0.02, z0.1) — its z_A gain (+1.68) is capped by typ_c (thoughts 0.43 nat/tok below the Qwen band,
    in-band 43 %), and the teacher loses exactly on z_A (-1.62): the arm re-targets the meter, it does not raise the ceiling.
 2. The only miner G3 rewards is the router's literal ask — Qwen thought + Genesis action — which tops teacher (+1.37, z6.4), king
    (+1.10) and Genesis itself (+1.35) with z_R flat (-0.15): the credit is for reproducing the pool member's action, not for a
    thought that leads to it; z_A never reads the miner's thought.
 3. Not routing-conditional: on bash, where the teacher beats Genesis on the env, the same arm gives Genesis +2.20 (z4.7) and the
    hybrid +2.66 over the teacher — larger than on tau2 — and teacher-failed vs teacher-solved tau2 tasks do not differ. The lift is
    family-similarity to the ref source (the RT-7 mechanism), so per-stratum routing from env verdicts buys nothing measurable.
 4. The control does not survive Genesis refs: teacher-king +0.83 (z6.5) under Q3 becomes -0.27 (z-2.1) under G3 on tau2 (text -0.55,
    z-3.3), -0.51 on bash; it holds under MIX (+0.54) — but MIX lifts every family by anchor degradation — and is weak under O3 (+0.18).
 5. Pool-member upload (RT-14) is binding wherever the member's thoughts are Qwen-typical (bash: Genesis tops by 2.2 z-units) and is
    blunted by the Qwen typ_c anchor where they are not (tau2); the sharper attack needs no upload: an ensemble/fine-tune that thinks
    like Qwen and acts like the member tops every pool arm, and near-deterministic member refs (self-exact 0.74-0.96) leave z_A as the
    whole meter.  Any implementation must also canonicalise the member's tool-call dialect (Genesis JSON vs Qwen XML) or it measures format.
 6. Occamy refs (O3) are the least harmful arm (teacher ~ Q3, control +0.18) because Occamy is a Qwen3.8 fine-tune whose actions agree
    with Qwen at Qwen's own self-agreement rate (0.31 vs 0.29) — i.e. they add little beyond Q3.  P3 (outcome-filtered Qwen refs) could
    not be tested; that arm, not a foreign-member pool, is where outcome information can enter without moving the ref family.
