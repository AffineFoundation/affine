Verdict
  1. In-band p_fr refs are NOT the uninformed ones: their decision-change rate (0.43) and judge SAME (0.50) sit near the out-of-band refs (0.50 / 0.48) and far from the blind floor (0.27 / 0.64–0.67); but they are the long, leak-light thoughts, and only 27% of refs / 6% of turns (3/3) qualify at k=3.
  2. In-band-only reference sets keep a large per-turn effect (R_own 0.020 vs blind-own 0.005, d/σ ≈ 0.9) but on 9 turns (hz 1.3) — coverage, not effect size, is the binding limit; requiring no-leak leaves 3 turns and no headroom.
  3. The paraphrase guard fails: leak 0.44 (unchanged), in-band per ref 0.25 → 0.14, ≥2 in-band clean refs on 10% of turns at N=6, and the clean-ref headroom collapses (hz 0.5 vs 1.8 for stored p_fr on the same turns) — the refs that restate the note are the ones that change the decision.
  4. Selection cannot be scaled by resampling alone: the projected N=6 coverage (≥3 in-band 0.21–0.27) is optimistic against the observed 0.017–0.10, because privileged thoughts sit ~5–6σ below the blind band as a class, not by chance.
  5. X2: the blind band does not separate verified-solved from failed teacher continuations (0.84 vs 0.85 in-band, z ≈ 0 both) — the band is a same-distribution test, not a quality test; solved teacher thoughts are already in band because every blind teacher thought is.
