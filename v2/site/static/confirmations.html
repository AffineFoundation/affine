Each applied crown under the next-round confirmation rule, its original paired z and delta, and the fresh paired test that decides payment. A crown is paid only if that test passes. Crowns before the rule are omitted. Bands, cumulative rows and total rates cover completed paid/unpaid tests only. Pending crowns await the next round; no_decision crowns had an infrastructure failure, are unpaid, and are excluded from rates. A historical paid row records that the crown passed its test, not that it remains in the current paid window.

This record is descriptive, not a rule change. Each crown is judged against the crown bar of the live params (z_crown when set, otherwise z_bar). The next-round confirmation is strict z > z_bar, the confirmation bar in the live params. Original z and confirmation z are rounded to 3 decimals, delta to 4 decimals, rates to integer percent.
