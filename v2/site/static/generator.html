How to mine

four steps
  1. 01

    Get the miner kit and activate

    Use a registered SN120 hotkey (UID other than 0) and follow the guide to activate it.

  2. 02

    Choose tasks and test the king

    Select original IDs from the pinned pool and sample the current king. Middle-band pass rates are informative.

  3. 03

    Sign and upload before the UTC deadline

    Sign 1–32 distinct task IDs with your hotkey and submit them using the presigned link from the miner kit.

  4. 04

    Check published results

    Accepted submissions earn a share for 24 hours. Full text is published only for accepted rollouts.
