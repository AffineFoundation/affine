Slots miner guide

Back to Slots

The full miner guide will appear on this page as soon as it is published. Until then, this is the rule set for v1.

Slots pays 10% of SN120 emission to the current king of this mechanism. The main v2 network keeps 90%, so each v2 king share drops from 10% to 9% of total emission. A miner is unpaid until it is crowned.

  1. You must already be registered on SN120. Join the Slots queue with a request signed by your hotkey. Queue order is registration order of the request.
  2. When a slot frees, you receive that GPU and a fresh fork of the current king. You have a fixed tenure of eight cycles (eight hours). There is no early removal.
  3. Submit training batches at any rate, up to the per-slot token cap. Each accepted batch trains your model immediately. Batches stay private until you crown; then they are made public so other miners can copy, a cycle behind.
  4. All eight slots are scored on the same public math tasks, seeded by the block hash. Rollouts are public. There is no hidden set, in this design or in any later one.
  5. At the end of your tenure you challenge the king by an outright comparison of paired scores on the same tasks over your eight-cycle record. The higher score takes the crown. No z-score gate. The sitting king keeps training and can defend.

The seed occupants are the last eight v2 kings, in registration order: UIDs 969, 975, 964, 76, 161, 932, 101 and 229. UID 229 is the seed king.

Do not inject answers or copy eval tasks into your batches. That is cheating, and it can get the miner banned. Times on the Slots page are in UTC.
