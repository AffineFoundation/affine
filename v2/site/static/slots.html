How it works

Miner guide

Slots is a live SN120 sub-mechanism. It runs eight miner models at once, one per GPU, and pays 10% of SN120 emission to the current king. The main v2 network keeps 90% of emission, so each v2 king share drops from 10% to 9% of the total.

  1. Registration. A miner already registered on SN120 joins the queue with a request signed by its hotkey. When a slot's tenure ends, the next miner in line takes that GPU, starting from a fresh fork of the current king.
  2. Batches. The occupant of a slot can submit training batches at any rate, up to a per-slot token cap. Each accepted batch trains that slot's model immediately. Batches stay private until that miner crowns; then they are made public.
  3. Evaluation. All eight slots are scored on the same public math tasks, drawn the same way as v2 and seeded by the block hash. Rollouts are public. There is no hidden set.
  4. Tenure. Every slot keeps its GPU for a fixed eight cycles (one cycle is one hour), regardless of score. There is no early removal.
  5. Crown. At the end of tenure the slot challenges the king by an outright comparison of paired scores on the same tasks over those eight cycles. The higher score takes the crown. The king keeps training and can defend. A miner is unpaid until it is crowned. If there is no king, or the state is stale, the 10% is burned.

The eight seed slots are the last eight v2 kings, in registration order: UIDs 969, 975, 964, 76, 161, 932, 101 and 229. UID 229 is the seed king.

Obvious overfitting in submitted batches, such as answer injection or copies of eval tasks, can lead to a ban. This page refreshes every 60 seconds. Times are in UTC.
