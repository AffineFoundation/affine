v1 history

archive

Subnet 120 ran v1 until the cutover to v2. v1 is shut down. This page shows the public history of v1 as it was at the cutover. It is a frozen copy: it does not change, and it needs no v1 service. The live subnet is on the v2 status page.

Kings when v1 stopped

—

The last distinct winner UIDs of v1, newest first, with their share of the miner weight. v2 starts with these kings.

Turns

—

One row per v1 turn, newest first. In a turn, v1 trained the batch of one miner and scored the candidate against the head. GREEN: the candidate was applied. An older turn has only its result, its UID, its task count and its candidate hash. The round files and the batch files of v1 are not part of this archive.

Benchmarks

—

Public math benchmarks of the v1 kings that had a benchmark run, newest first, scores in percent. Under the kings: the reference models, best average first, and the v1 genesis model. The cards show each score over the v1 kings. A star marks the highest score of a column. † marks a partial run: some samples were lost. For information only.
