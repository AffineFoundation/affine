About Affine

Bittensor subnet 120 · open-weight model competition

Affine is Bittensor subnet 120 (SN120): a king-of-the-hill competition for open-weight language models. Miners upload fine-tuned checkpoints; the Affine validator duels each challenger against the reigning king on held-out agent trajectories and scores both with one teacher-anchored distillation metric — how well the model's own reasoning predicts a fixed teacher model's next action — never an LLM judge. The winner is crowned king, earns the subnet's emissions, and its weights are published and served openly. This site is the live validator dashboard.
