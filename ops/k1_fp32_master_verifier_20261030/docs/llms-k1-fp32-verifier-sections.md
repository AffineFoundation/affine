# Sections added to https://affine.io/llms.txt (K=1 section), 2026-10-30

**fp32 head master (from epoch 274).** The trainer now keeps a persistent fp32 master copy of the head. The step is the same one AdamW step from zero moments (lr 5e-7, per-tensor relative cap 2e-5, no moments carried), but it is taken on the head's fp32 master M_H instead of fp32(bf16 head): `d_t = lr * (g_t/(|g_t|+1e-8) + 0.01*M_H,t*[2-D, not bias])`, `r_t = min(1, 2e-5*(1-1e-6)*||M_H,t||/||d_t||)`, `M_C = M_H - fp32(s*r_t)*d_t`, and the bf16 candidate is `bf16_round_nearest_even(M_C)`. Small updates that round away in bf16 are kept in the master and accumulate across GREEN turns instead of being lost. RED candidates are discarded with their master; a GREEN makes M_C the new head master. The first v8.3.3f turn (epoch 274) starts from the exact fp32 upcast of the bf16 head. Each turn commits `training.optimizer.sgd.fp32_master` in turn.json: `input_master_digest` / `output_master_digest` (sha256 of the canonical JSON `{tensor name: sha256(little-endian fp32 bytes)}`), the bf16 sha256s and the master source (`bootstrap-upcast`, `memory` or `disk`). On every GREEN the new head master is published: safetensors shards plus `meta.json` in R2 under `checkpoints/<C>/fp32-master/` (same bucket and access as `checkpoints/<C>/model/`), with a manifest at `https://affine.io/k1/fp32-master/<C>.json` and an index at `https://affine.io/k1/fp32-master/index.json`. A replay of turn N needs the head master from the previous GREEN's manifest (or the bf16 upcast for the bootstrap turn); `bf16_rne(master)` must equal the published bf16 head. Recipe: `https://affine.io/k1/spec/step-recipe.json` -> `optimizer.fp32_master`.

**Verifier fix (gate grading, from epoch 274).** The gate's `affine_math` grade now also accepts correct answers the strict projection or `verify.py` scored 0 (Allan's fix, `catoneone/sn120-k1-fixes` 786cdb9, `code/lenient_grader.py`, rules P1/P2/E1-E7: a bare box after a closed `$$`, trailing text or several boxes (the last complete box is graded), `\text{}`/thousands separators, multiple-choice letters mapped to their option, pmatrix vs tuple, single-variable inequalities vs intervals, `kI` vs an explicit matrix, `i` as the imaginary unit). It is applied only when the live grade is not positive and the reply ended with EOS; capped replies stay unresolved, and a live positive is never changed. On the 4,073 finished K=1 rollouts of epochs 196-260 it recovers 481 correct answers and changes no wrong answer. It applies to the head and candidate runs alike (it is part of the probe worker). The open held-out round was closed when it went live, and a fresh round with a new baseline started at epoch 274 on the same head. **Miner-side grading (the native grades in your batch and the trainer reward) is unchanged for now**: they must match the validator's native grader, so that changes only with a coordinated client release.
