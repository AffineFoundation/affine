Baseline

Each gate round (one per head) scores candidates against the head on a fresh random draw of about 500 tasks from the 19,305-task baseline pool. The draw is not uniform: a task’s chance of being picked grows with p(1 − p), where p is the head’s pass rate on it, so tasks the head solves about half the time are picked most often and tasks it always or never solves are picked least (a small uniform floor keeps every task in play). As the head improves, more tasks move toward a pass rate of 1 and the draw shifts toward the tasks it still finds hard.
