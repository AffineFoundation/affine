# Prospective confirmed-blacklist training selection

This mechanism is default-off and is not deployed. It changes training supply only; it does not wait for every sample audit, relabel trajectories, or change audit/reward eligibility. New miners, UNKNOWN numerical outcomes and infrastructure failures remain eligible.

A ROOT-signed `learner_blacklist_selection_authorization` in coordinator configuration approves `automatic-confirmed-blacklist-training-selection-v1`, the exact scientific `source_sha256`, current `writer_policy_sha256`, `audit_policy`, bounded `maximum_age_seconds` (1–7200), and an ordinary owned `assessment_path`. Each new opening reads the latest already ROOT-signed, non-stale hourly assessment and authenticates its writer binding. Under the approved mechanism the local coordinator signs a new `learner_blacklist_selection_policy` pinned to that opening's checkpoint and protected `learner_blacklist_selection_round`. Saved openings never refresh this snapshot. No per-epoch manual ROOT signing is needed after mechanism approval.

The selection journal freezes the snapshot/policy hashes, excluded identities/documents and original random draw. Only explicit policy-blacklisted identities with sufficient confirmed-invalid evidence and an unexpired target-round duration are removed from the training candidate pool before the 256 cap. Invalid, unavailable, stale or wrong-source/round evidence refuses the enabled operation; it never silently opens eligibility. Retry uses the original captured selection time and snapshot, not the later hourly assessment.

The complete structural eligible inventory and continuous audit/reward population remain unchanged. Receipts distinguish `eligible_count`, `training_eligible_count`, `blacklist_excluded_count`, selected inputs and unselected training candidates. The signed training manifest carries the original snapshot and protected round; source-pinned job validation prevents reinsertion of excluded inputs or substitution of a different snapshot. The input inventory, source/policy computation binding and existing same-job optimizer resume remain authoritative.

Historical manifests without the field keep their original selection, coverage and receipt format. Historical blacklist status is not a new candidate fraud verdict. This mechanism does not resolve disputed TOPLOC observations itself; it consumes the existing effective assessment after authorized numerical adjudication. Activation requires ROOT review of the automatic authorization, exact CPU/runtime module pins, opening/job admission and deployment compatibility. Existing frozen remote science must not be represented as containing these new module bytes.
