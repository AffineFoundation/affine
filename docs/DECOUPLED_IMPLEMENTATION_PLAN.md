# Decoupled learning and continuous audits

The operator authorized implementation, parallel agent work, and necessary H200
rentals on 2026-10-05. The goal is active. This document describes the target;
it does not claim that the new policy is deployed.

## Parallel ownership

- Learner/controller: cheap admission of committed token documents, explicit
  unaudited provenance, training and durable checkpoint publication without an
  audit barrier. Preserve the separate strict historical recovery path.
- Auditor: fast teacher-forced prefill checks, calibrated numerical ambiguity,
  continuous random audits, evidence deduplication, recent-cohort validity
  estimates, and versioned penalty/weight snapshots.
- Deployment: four additional single-H200 verifiers, qualification, miner
  commitment transport and immutable capture, compatible deployment documentation.
- Root: contract integration, review, source qualification, authority signatures,
  deployment, GitHub/public documentation compatibility and measured acceptance.

## Contract and deployment sequence

1. Bind small canonical token-only training documents and separate proof objects
   to signed miner commitments, checkpoint, task, identity and epoch. Enforce
   bounded sizes and reject held-out tasks in cheap admission.
2. Introduce explicit new learning and fast-audit policy versions. Existing strict
   historical requests retain their original meaning and optimizer parent.
3. Test honest data, mutations, replayed evidence, numerical boundary cases,
   infrastructure failures and checkpoint transitions. Ambiguity is not fraud.
4. Qualify all new verifier runtimes and register them through reviewed authority
   grants. A rented machine is not an enrolled verifier.
5. Deploy a coherent pinned source and matching public llms.txt. Advance the
   checkpoint only after durable checkpoint and trainer-state publication.
6. Demonstrate successive real epochs with background audits and independent
   held-out evaluation; record stage timings, throughput and reward calculations.

## Completion evidence

Training must advance without waiting for expensive audits, while audit evidence
continues to update reproducible reward snapshots. Show measured throughput and
held-out learning, with monitoring and recoverable checkpoint lineage. Increase
capacity only after those core mechanics work. Do not claim that sampling proves
every unaudited batch correct or that a finite test proves freedom from exploits.

Local cache lifecycle improvements remain explicitly deferred.
