# Two-success/two-failure trainer cutover

This candidate supports the requested K=2/L=2 batch: four distinct rollouts for one task, paired in original class order into exactly two disjoint preference pairs. It does not construct four Cartesian pairs. Persistent task-normalized training averages these two pair losses within the task, then averages tasks within the optimizer update. Extra rollouts therefore do not multiply a task's reward or gradient weight. Adam hyperparameters, restored FP32 master/momentum lineage, learning rate, optimizer steps, coverage scheduling and durable publication remain unchanged.

The worker and independent report validator activate strict two-pair population checks only for signed K=L=2 manifests. They reject incomplete tasks, extra pairs, exact cloned pairs, and a positive or negative token trajectory shared across the two pairs, ignoring metadata. Historical K=1/L=1 behavior stays on the original path. The new scientific source requires fresh source qualification; old jobs and historical approvals must retain their original bytes.

The CPU native-label selector requires limits.version `bounded-native-training-label-filter-k2l2-v2` for K=L=2. Its maximum is 512 pairs (256 selected task documents × two pairs), still four grader processes maximum with independent per-grade and wall deadlines. The v1 policy retains its 256-pair cap and cannot authorize K2/L2. All four original outputs must have determinate, matching native labels and valid terminal framing: one failing/indeterminate pair excludes the entire task document, without changing labels, assigning cheating penalties or calling an inference verifier. Signed subset validation demands exactly two dispositions per document. Inference audit completion remains outside the training eligibility path.
