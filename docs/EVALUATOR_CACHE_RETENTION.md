Evaluator roles now retain their current approved checkpoint and retire registered obsolete cache inventories before a future job and after terminal execution. Checkpoint locks are inherited by original backend children. Unknown files, reports, source trees, credentials, partial current weights and active leases remain protected. Postterminal cleanup errors are recorded separately and do not change scientific job success.

Historical evaluator caches require explicit ROOT-signed `owned-evaluator-cache-catalog-v1` ownership, a current checkpoint exclusion, signed durable checkpoint inventories, quiescent original PID evidence, and full local file hashes. The adoption utility registers exact owned paths and invokes normal CacheLifecycle retention. It never scans and deletes unknown directories. A failed ENOSPC evaluation remains failed; future diagnostics require a distinct reviewed request and genuine baseline.
